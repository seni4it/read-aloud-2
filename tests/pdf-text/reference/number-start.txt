The first trial of the method began in the spring, and it ran from nineteen ninety-six to two thousand four in three clinics. Each clinic measured the same teeth with a gauge that reads to zero point five millimetres, and the mean depth was two point five millimetres at the start of the trial. The team counted 120 patients in the first year and 340 patients in the last year. Class two cavities were the most common finding, and Class four cavities were rare. The report has two short lists of findings. 1. The depth did not change in the first two years.
2. The depth became less after the change of method.
