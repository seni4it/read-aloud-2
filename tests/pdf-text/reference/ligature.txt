This page tests the repair of words that lost letters. The font of some books draws two letters as one sign, and the P D F has no letter for that sign. The reader must put the letters back. The words emotions and condition lost the letters t and i in this way. The words emotions and unconditional lost the same letters with no mark at all. A short word such as the or and must stay as it is, and a real word such as station must also stay as it is.
