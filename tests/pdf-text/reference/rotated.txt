This page is a landscape page. Its text is turned a quarter, and the page is turned back a quarter, so the text stands upright on the screen. In the file, each line runs up the sheet and the next line sits to its right. Each line here is drawn in two pieces, so a reader that sorts the pieces by their height on the sheet mixes the lines. A good reader turns the page first: the reader must read each line from its start to its end, and the lines from the top down.
