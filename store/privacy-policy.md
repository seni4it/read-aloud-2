# Read Aloud 2: privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

Read Aloud 2 is a Chrome extension that reads web pages, PDFs and pictures aloud.

## What it collects

Nothing. The extension has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

## What happens to the text you listen to

The text of the page, PDF or picture you choose to hear is processed only on your own computer: by the voices built into your computer and Chrome, or by the optional voice server running on your own Mac at the address 127.0.0.1. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What is stored

Your settings (voice, speed, on/off switches) are kept in Chrome's own storage. The PDF reader remembers your reading position in each document on your computer.
