Send your audiobooks to your private YouTube channel

You do this one time. It takes about 10 minutes. Google asks for these steps; the app cannot do them for you.

  1. Open Google Cloud. Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the Google account of your YouTube channel.
  2. Make a project. At the top, click the project name (or Select a project). Click New project. Name it Read Aloud. Click Create. Wait, then select it.
  3. Turn on the YouTube API. In the left menu, click APIs & Services → Library. Search for YouTube Data API v3. Open it and click Enable.
  4. Set the consent screen. Click APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen. Choose External. App name: Read Aloud. Fill in your e-mail address in the two e-mail fields. Click Save and continue until the end.
  5. Add yourself as a test user. On the consent screen page, find Test users (or Audience). Click Add users, type your e-mail address, click Save.
  6. Make the key. Click APIs & Services → Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID. Application type: Desktop app. Name: Read Aloud. Click Create. In the box that opens, click Download JSON. The file goes to your Downloads folder.
  7. Give the key to the app. Go back to the Library. Click YouTube at the top. Click Choose the key file and pick the file that you downloaded.
  8. Connect. Click Connect to YouTube. Google opens. Choose your account. Google says the app is not verified: click Continue. Click Allow. The page then says "YouTube is connected".

Two limits from Google. First: about 6 uploads a day. The app sends the chapters by itself, 6 each day, while this Mac is on. Second: videos longer than 15 minutes need a verified account. Open youtube.com/verify one time and confirm your phone number.

After this, each book that you export as MP3 goes to YouTube by itself, as private videos with the cover picture, in a playlist with the name of the book. Only you can see them.
